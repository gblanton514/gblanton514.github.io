Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-25

This application is a private, internal tool built and used solely by TDG Energy Services to read accounting data (such as bills, purchases, and vendor records) from our own QuickBooks Online company for internal project cost reconciliation and financial analysis.

What data we access

This application connects only to TDG Energy Services' own QuickBooks Online company, using credentials authorized directly by an employee of TDG Energy Services. It reads accounting records (bills, purchases, vendors, invoices) and does not create, modify, or delete any data in QuickBooks.

How data is used

Data retrieved from QuickBooks is used only for internal financial reconciliation and reporting within TDG Energy Services. It is not sold, shared, or disclosed to any third party.

Who can use this application

This application is for internal use by authorized TDG Energy Services personnel only. It is not published or made available to the public or to any other company.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to TDG Energy Services.
